        ~ the early stages for evil players is to be in contrived challenges where they are put ina  situation where they are baited with taking on the evil plan

        ~ good guys want to stop it, bad guys want to take advantage
    - 2nd and 3rd quarter of the game is
        ~ bad guys trying to work on their plan in secret
        ~ good guys trying to work together, investigate, gain more info, and build a strategy
