- gust of icy wind
  - each time it his the players move speed is reduced by 90%
  - minor counter is to stay near warmth
  - major counter cold weather clothes

- mud / tar puddles
  - each time applies diminishing slow
  - minor counter to clean off
  - major counter, to set plan to look for puddles and evade them

- overgrowth plants
  - each time walking through them slows you down
  - minor fix to evade
  - major fix to cut back the plants

- sandstorm
  - hurts eyes and no vision
  - minor stay indoors
  - major goggles
