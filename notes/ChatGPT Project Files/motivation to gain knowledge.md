> A recursive **clarity-mystery loop**, where the player knows _just enough_ to pursue a goal, but also sees _exactly what they don’t know_ — and is **driven to find it** because they know why it matters.

## 🧭 DESIGN GOAL:

> Make the **final showdown** structure _clearly visible_, but make its _subsystems_ **obfuscated but earnable**.

The player should feel:

> “I know how to win… but I **need more information** to ensure I do.”

---

## ✅ THE SOLUTION: Dual-layer Final Showdown Design

| Layer              | What It Contains                                           | Clarity Level          | How It Motivates the Player                                                                  |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Outer Layer**    | Final showdown **framework** & basic win condition logic   | 🔍 Full                | “Here’s the arena, here's how someone can win.”                                              |
| **Inner Layer(s)** | Subsystems, interactions, traps, node chains, dependencies | 🔒 Hidden (but hinted) | “I can tell something's missing here… I need to understand how this mechanism really works.” |

---

So the player:

- Understands the rules
- Sees an unsolved variable
- Is _driven_ to obtain the missing info
- Now has a reason to _care_ about the Tier 2 opportunity

---

## 🧩 Now Build Your Knowledge Tree Like a Lock-and-Key Network

Imagine a visible **checklist of known unknowns**, like:

### 📜 “Unresolved Final Showdown Mechanics” (player's perspective)

- [ ] What causes Phase 2 threat escalation?
- [ ] What cancels node lockout in Phase 1?
- [x] How to delay Phase 3 trigger (already discovered)
- [ ] Who else has Knowledge7?

Each item:

- Is _clearly labeled_ (so players know what to seek)
- Is _connectable_ to tiers or opportunities (so players know where to look)

This creates **intentional discovery**, not random stumbling.

---

## 🔄 Recap: How to Achieve Your Goal

| What You Want                                                         | How to Build It                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Players clearly see the **structure** of the final showdown           | Make the map/flow of the final showdown public from early on                                         |
| Players know their **goal** is to manipulate it into a winning state  | Show them how starting the win-sequence works, and that the state determines the outcome             |
| Players see **obvious missing information**                           | Design showdown nodes with gaps/hints — use "rule-based unknowns"                                    |
| Players know that specific **opportunities unlock that info**         | Use a lock/key structure: “Knowledge7 unlocks Node4. Knowledge7 is earned from Opportunity4”         |
| Players actively **seek out knowledge** as part of strategic planning | Let them see a list of unknowns. Or, in dialogue/UI/logs, remind them: “you’re missing Knowledge X…” |

---

## 🧠 Optional UX Mechanic: "Knowledge Gaps Display"

A simple UI or narrative mechanic:

- A “Final Showdown Planner” screen
- Shows all visible showdown nodes
- For each node, shows **what knowledge is missing**
- You can only interact meaningfully with the node once you have that knowledge

This makes the player go:

> “I _know_ I need Knowledge4… where can I get it?”

Now, that **missing knowledge becomes the next quest**.
